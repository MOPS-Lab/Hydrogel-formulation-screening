Source-aware Gaussian process · Maxwell-derived modulus G · model v6, repository release 2026-11-09

Exploratory, conditional predictions. Estimates assume that a gel forms. Candidate recipes have not been experimentally validated. Monte Carlo ranges describe assumed preparation perturbations through a fixed model; they are not prediction intervals or synthesis-success probabilities.

1. Predict from a formulation

Inputs use nominal additive 1:1 polymer-mixture : crosslinker-solution volumes. Final polymer concentration = stock concentration × liquid fraction ÷ 2; final ion dose = crosslinker stock dose ÷ 2. These are model coordinates, not measured internal gel concentrations.

Stock inputs: 0.1–3 wt.%; unused stock values do not contribute. Liquid fractions must sum to 1. Fe and Al denote the trivalent salts. The dilution divisor stays 2 for every ion.

Experimental context and model limits

pH 7; crosslinking for 24 h at 5 °C; rheology at 25 °C. TOCNF carboxyl content 1.5 mmol/g dry matter; alginate G fraction 70%. These conditions are fixed metadata, not learned variables. The experimental protocol describes spraying/bath crosslinking; the nominal volume conversion does not establish homogeneous mixing or equilibrium ion concentrations.

The final GP uses 198 positive observations. Seven recorded failures are exact-match exclusions, not a learned gelation classifier. For ion-free predictions the historical source category is used; other ions use the new-series category. Mg has no new-series validation.

2. Preparation Monte Carlo

Independent uniform perturbations of the formulation above. Stock half-widths are absolute percentage points; dispensed-volume and crosslinker half-widths are relative percentages.

3. Search candidate recipes

Uses the target, tolerance and Monte Carlo settings above. Equal active polymer stocks are searched from 1 to 3 wt.% in steps of 0.1; fractions use steps of 0.05. Dose is searched on a grid and locally refined. This is a restricted search, with no global-optimum guarantee.

A nominal recipe inside the support can produce perturbations outside it. The support indicator is geometric and does not guarantee accurate prediction.

Exploratory MCMC comparison

MCMC is separate from the retained GP. Nominal 80% intervals covered 18/30 observations (60%) in grouped validation. The browser uses 32 representative hyperparameter draws; validation uses 128. Intervals are not calibrated for a new recipe.
