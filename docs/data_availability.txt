The retained formulation dataset, record-level provenance, historical benchmark summaries and recovered Ca/no-ion Gaussian-process implementation, source-aware Gaussian-process model, executable new-series validation and comparator analyses, preparation Monte Carlo and MCMC code, archived posterior samples, candidate-search outputs, and an offline interactive formulation-screening tool are available at https://github.com/MOPS-Lab/Hydrogel-formulation-screening.
